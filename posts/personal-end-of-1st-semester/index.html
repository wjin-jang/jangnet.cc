Personal/End of First Semester

February 26, 2026 · Personal, FMCC, ICP

First semester at UAL is finished. I'm two weeks into my second semester and now have the results for my first set of assignments. Straight "A+"s across the board. Not bad, if I say so myself. I suppose my eagerness to share says something about how I feel about it.

Overall, I'm liking my time at UAL so far. The course is good, if a little unchallenging, though I do try to challenge myself in everything I do. One of the main things I've done this semester is try to learn things beyond the course requirements. I've practiced physical computing, laser cutting, shaders, Godot, 3D modelling, etc. Everything here is really conducive to that kind of learning. My access to tools and knowledge is really unprecedented at this point in my life.

My teachers have all been great. I've gotten a lot of good feedback, positive feedback but also good criticism. In this semester I need to focus on more rigorous academic engagement, looking at other artists' work. I'm reading 'Artificial Hells' at the moment, a critique of participatory art and the ways it is judged. I'm also trying to level up my skills in coding and aesthetic judgement, for myself of course, but always critiquing and learning from others too.

I suppose this does put the pressure on to keep the good scores going. I'll do my best. And stay humble.

Thanks,
Woojin Jang
